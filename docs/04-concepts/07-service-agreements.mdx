**Service Agreements** allow you to create, customize, and enforce multi-party service agreements directly within Eneris. By requiring clients to sign agreements before inspections, you establish liability protection and ensure all parties understand the scope and terms of your services.

Service Agreements are critical for:

- **Liability protection** — Ensure agreements are signed before performing inspections.
- **Multi-signature support** — Handle unmarried buyers, siblings, investors, and other multi-party scenarios.
- **Workflow efficiency** — Eliminate the need for external tools like DocuSign or paper-based agreements.

:::info

Service Agreement Templates are reusable across multiple [**Form Templates**](/docs/concepts/form-templates). Set them up once and apply them to any template you create.

:::

## What is a Service Agreement?

A **Service Agreement** is a legally binding document that defines the terms and conditions of your inspection services. It outlines what you will and will not inspect, your liability limitations, and other important terms that protect both you and your clients.

Including a signed Service Agreement with your inspections helps:

- Protect you from liability claims
- Set clear expectations about the scope of the inspection
- Provide documentation that clients acknowledged and agreed to your terms

## Managing Service Agreement Templates

### View Service Agreement Templates

To view your existing **Service Agreement Templates**:

1. Select the hamburger menu in the upper left within the app on your mobile device.
2. Select `Service Agreement templates`.

{/* <img src={ImageServiceAgreements1} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="Service Agreement Templates menu in sidebar." /> */}

You will see a list of all available Service Agreement Templates, including any templates you have created.

{/* <img src={ImageServiceAgreements2} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="List of Service Agreement templates." /> */}

### Create a Service Agreement Template

To create a new **Service Agreement Template**:

1. Select the hamburger menu in the upper left within the app on your mobile device.
2. Select `Service Agreement templates`.
3. Select `Create new Service Agreement template`.
4. Enter a **Name** for your template (this is for internal reference only).
5. Enter a **Title** (this appears in your PDF reports).
6. Enter the agreement content in the **Body** field.
7. Optionally, insert **Auto-fill tags** to dynamically populate inspection-specific information.
8. Select `Save Changes` at the bottom of the page.

{/* <img src={ImageServiceAgreements3} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="Create new Service Agreement template form." /> */}

:::info

The **Name** field is used to identify the template in your list. The **Title** field is what appears in your client-facing PDF reports.

:::

### Edit a Service Agreement Template

To modify an existing **Service Agreement Template**:

1. Navigate to `Service Agreement templates` from the hamburger menu.
2. Select the template you wish to edit.
3. Update the **Name**, **Title**, and **Body** fields as needed.
4. Select `Save changes`.

:::info

Changes to a **Service Agreement Template** only affect new [**Inspections**](/docs/concepts/inspections) created after the change. Existing inspections retain the Service Agreement content that was embedded when they were created.

:::

{/* <img src={ImageServiceAgreements4} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="Edit Service Agreement template showing title and body fields." /> */}

### Delete a Service Agreement Template

To delete a **Service Agreement Template**:

1. Navigate to `Service Agreement templates` from the hamburger menu.
2. Select the template you wish to delete.
3. Scroll to the bottom and select `Delete Service Agreement Template`.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

:::warning

Deleting a Service Agreement Template cannot be undone. Existing inspections that use this template will retain their embedded Service Agreement content.

:::

## Auto-Fill Tags

**Auto-fill tags** allow you to dynamically insert inspection-specific information into your Service Agreement Templates. When a template is applied to an inspection, these tags are automatically replaced with the actual values.

| Tag | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `{{CLIENT}}` | Client's contact name |
| `{{INSPECTOR}}` | Inspector's name |
| `{{BUSINESS_NAME}}` | Your business name |
| `{{ADDRESS}}` | Inspection address |
| `{{DATE}}` | Inspection date |
| `{{CLIENT_ADDRESS}}` | Client's current address |
| `{{SUBTOTAL}}` | Invoice subtotal |

:::tip

Use the dropdown menu when editing your template to select and insert auto-fill tags. You can preview how tags will appear before saving.

:::

:::info

When a Service Agreement Template is applied to an inspection, auto-fill tags are converted to actual text values. This conversion is one-way — the tags cannot be reverted once applied.

:::

## Inspector Signature

Your signature appears on all Service Agreements you send for signing. To set up your inspector signature:

1. Navigate to your [**Inspector Profile**](/docs/concepts/inspector-profiles).
2. Locate the **Signature** field.
3. Draw or upload your signature.
4. Save your profile.

Your signature is stored securely and will automatically appear on all Service Agreements associated with your inspections.

:::info

Your inspector signature is a one-time setup. Once configured, it applies to all Service Agreements across all inspections.

:::

## Linking Service Agreements to Form Templates

To associate a **Service Agreement Template** with a [**Form Template**](/docs/concepts/form-templates):

1. Navigate to `Form templates` from the hamburger menu.
2. Select the **Form Template** you wish to modify.
3. Locate the **Service Agreement** field.
4. Select the edit icon then select the desired **Service Agreement template** from the menu.
5. Navigate back a page then select `Save` in the upper right.

{/* <img src={ImageServiceAgreements5} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="Linking Service Agreement template to a Form Template." /> */}

:::info

Once linked, the Service Agreement is automatically embedded when creating new [**Inspections**](/docs/concepts/inspections) using that **Form Template**. The agreement content is copied into the Inspection Form, allowing for per-inspection modifications if needed.

:::

## Service Agreements in Inspection Forms

When you create an [**Inspection**](/docs/concepts/inspections) using a **Form Template** that has a Service Agreement linked, the agreement content is automatically embedded into the [**Inspection Form**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms).

### Applying a Service Agreement

You have several options when working with Service Agreements in an Inspection Form:

- **Apply a template** — Select from your existing Service Agreement Templates
- **Write from scratch** — Create a one-off agreement for this specific inspection
- **Select a different template** — Switch to a different template if needed

:::info

Each inspection can have a maximum of one Service Agreement.

:::

### Making Ad-Hoc Changes

You can modify the Service Agreement content directly within an Inspection Form to make inspection-specific changes. This is useful when:

- A client requests additional scope clarifications
- You need to add project-specific limitations
- You want to customize terms for a particular situation

Changes made to the Service Agreement within an **Inspection Form** only affect that specific Inspection — they do not update the original **Service Agreement Template**.

:::warning

Making changes to a Service Agreement after it has been signed will clear all existing signatures. Clients will need to re-sign the updated agreement.

:::

### Viewing Signature Status

The Service Agreement section displays the current signature status, showing how many parties have signed (e.g., "2/3 signatures completed"). This allows you to quickly see if any signatures are still pending.

### Sending for Signature

To send a Service Agreement for signature:

1. Open the Inspection Form containing your Service Agreement.
2. Navigate to the Service Agreement section.
3. Select `Request signatures` from the context menu.
4. Choose which contacts need to sign.
5. Select whether to collect signatures in-person or send signing requests via email.

## Multi-Signature Support

Eneris supports up to **10 signatures** on a single Service Agreement, accommodating complex scenarios such as:

- Unmarried couples purchasing together
- Siblings or family members as co-buyers
- Investors or business partners
- Any combination of multiple parties

### Configuring Signers

When requesting signatures, you select which contacts from the inspection need to sign. Only contacts associated with the inspection can be added as signers.

Signatures are arranged in two columns in the final document to save space, with the inspector's signature appearing last.

### In-Person Signing

For in-person signing:

1. Select the signer from the contact list.
2. Hand the device to the signer.
3. A full-page signature pad opens for signing.
4. The signer draws their signature and confirms.

### Off-Site Signing

For off-site signing:

1. Request signatures via email.
2. Each signer receives a personalized link to sign.
3. Signers can only sign above their own name.
4. Signatures are mapped to contact email addresses for verification.

### Managing Signatures

If a signature needs to be corrected:

1. Navigate to the Service Agreement in the Inspection Form.
2. Locate the signature that needs correction.
3. Delete the individual signature.
4. Request a new signature from that party.

:::info

Signatures are tied to the Service Agreement. If the agreement is deleted, all associated signatures are also removed.

:::

## Electronic Signing Interface

The signing interface works across all platforms:

**Web:**
- Sign using your mouse or trackpad
- Full signature pad interface

**Mobile:**
- Sign using your finger
- Optimized for touch screens

**Features:**
- **Cursive pre-filled option** — Generate a cursive signature based on the signer's name
- **Free-form drawing** — Draw your signature manually
- **Delete and re-do** — Clear and restart if you make a mistake

## Signature Notifications

### For Signers

After successfully signing a Service Agreement, signers receive an email confirmation acknowledging their signature has been recorded.

### For Inspectors

When all required parties have signed the Service Agreement, you receive an email notification confirming the agreement is fully executed.

## Service Agreement in Reports

When you [publish a report](/docs/concepts/reports#publishing-a-report), the Service Agreement appears in the PDF report.

**Placement:**
- Second-to-last section (before Standards of Practice)
- Added to the Table of Contents

**Content includes:**
- **Title** — The agreement title you specified
- **Body** — The full agreement text
- **Signatures** — All collected signatures with printed names

{/* <img src={ImageServiceAgreements6} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="Service Agreement section in PDF report." /> */}

:::info

If additional signatures are collected after the report is published, you must manually re-publish the report to include the new signatures.

:::

## Standalone PDF

You can generate and share a fully signed Service Agreement as a standalone PDF document, separate from the inspection report.

To generate a standalone PDF:

1. Open the Inspection Form containing your Service Agreement.
2. Navigate to the Service Agreement section.
3. Select `Share PDF` from the context menu.
4. Choose how to share the document (email, messaging, etc.).

This is useful when clients request a copy of just the signed agreement without the full inspection report.
