# Syncing

Syncing ensures that your inspection data is uploaded to the cloud, so it’s backed up and accessible across all your devices.

Syncing sends the latest version of your [**Inspection Forms**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms) from your local device to the Eneris cloud. This keeps your data safe, allows for collaboration, and enables seamless access to your **Inspection Forms** from other devices.

:::warning Important

If an **Inspection Form** has not been synced for more than 30 days, any unsynced changes will be lost. To avoid losing your work, sync your forms regularly.

:::

## Auto-syncing

Any **out-of-sync** offline forms will automatically sync in the background at a regular interval if the app is open and you are connected to the internet.

The app will also download any inspection forms that are within the last 30 days or newer to your device if they are not already on your device.

You can also **manually sync** each form by selecting the **Sync** option in the form properties.

## Syncing vs. Saving

While saving stores your work locally on your mobile device, **syncing** uploads the report to the cloud for backup and access across devices.

| Action | Where it’s stored | When it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saving | On your local mobile device | Every 30 seconds or manually |
| Syncing | In the Eneris cloud | Automatically in the background while the app is active. You may also manually sync. |

:::info

For more information on **Saving** [click here](/docs/concepts/saving).

:::
