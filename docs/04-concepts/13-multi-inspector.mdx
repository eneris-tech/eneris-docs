# Multi-Inspector

Eneris enables multiple inspectors to work together. The platform is designed for inspection companies or teams that need visibility across schedules, inspections, and reports while maintaining clear roles and responsibilities.

Individuals with the **Admin** or **Scheduler** roles can:

- Assign different inspectors to individual inspections
- Assign multiple inspectors to the same inspection
- Track each inspector's workload using a shared calendar
- Keep inspection data and reports organized by team member
- Enable collaboration while maintaining accountability

Whether you're a small team or a growing business, Eneris helps you stay coordinated and efficient.

## Team

A **Team** is defined as all individuals who have been invited to access some, or all, of your organization's inspection data in Eneris.

## Team owner

A **Team owner** is a single individual who is the main point of contact between Eneris and your organization.

## Multi-inspector configuration

Learn about multi-inspector configuration.

:::info

Teams and roles can only be edited by those with the **Admin** role.

:::

### Manage accounts and roles

In the settings menu, locate the `Team & access` section and select `Manage accounts and roles`.

You will then see a list of team members.

- Select each team member to edit their role or delete their account.
- Select the edit button to add new team members, edit the team owner, or delete the team.

## Syncing for multi-inspector

Eneris allows multiple inspectors to inspect the same property and combine their insights together into a single cohesive report.

The platform smartly merges each inspectors' insights when their [**Inspection Forms**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms) are [synced](/docs/concepts/syncing).

:::tip

Each inspector will need to sync and refresh the [**Inspection Form**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms) to view the latest updates from their colleagues. See [syncing](/docs/concepts/syncing) for more information.

:::
