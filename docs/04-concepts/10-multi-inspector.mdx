# Multi-Inspector

Multi-inspector mode allows multiple inspectors to work together. It’s designed for inspection companies or teams that need visibility across schedules, inspections, and reports while maintaining clear roles and responsibilities.

With multi-inspector enabled, administrators and schedulers can:

- Assign different inspectors to individual inspections
- Assign multiple inspectors to the same inspection
- Track each inspector’s workload using a shared calendar
- Keep inspection data and reports organized by team member
- Enable collaboration while maintaining accountability

Whether you’re a small team or a growing business, multi-inspector mode helps you stay coordinated and efficient.

## Teams and Team Owners

Definitions of a **Team** and a **Team Owner** in Eneris.

### Team

A **Team** is defined as all individuals who have been invited to access some, or all, of your organization’s inspection data in Eneris.

### Team owner

A **Team owner** is a single individual who is the main point of contact between Eneris and your organization.

## Configuring multi-inspector mode

Learn how to setup multi-inspector mode.

:::info

Multi-inspector mode can only be setup by those with the **Admin** role.

:::

### Manage accounts and roles

In the settings menu, locate the `Team & access` section and select `Manage accounts and roles`.

You will then see a list of team members.

- Select each team member to edit their role or delete their account.
- Select the edit button to add new team members, edit the team owner, or delete the team.

## Syncing for multi-inspector

Multi-inspector mode allows multiple inspectors to inspect the same property and combine their insights together into a single cohesive report.

Eneris smartly merges each inspectors’ insights when their insights are [synced](/docs/concepts/syncing).

:::tip

Each inspector will need to sync and refresh the inspection to view the latest updates from their colleagues. Take a look at [syncing](/docs/concepts/syncing) for more information.

:::
