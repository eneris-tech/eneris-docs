# Inspection Forms

An **Inspection Form** is where the **bulk of your inspection data is entered and organized**. It contains all the categories, components, fields, and field options used to document your entries during an inspection.

Each form acts as a structured workspace, allowing you to:

- Record detailed entries (e.g. roof is in good condition, mold on interior wall, water heater needs replacement, etc.)
- Attach media (photos, videos)
- Select field options or enter free-form notes
- Add general limitations to your inspection
- Customize what's shown in the final report

Whether you're performing a home inspection, mold inspection, asbestos inspection — or any other type of inspection — **Inspection Forms** keep your data organized, clear, and report-ready.

## How Form Templates and Inspection Forms are related

[**Form Templates**](/docs/concepts/form-templates) allow for the customization of inspection lists, and the pre-filling of report fields. Once saved, they are available for selection when creating a new [**Inspection**](/docs/concepts/inspections),

<img src={ImageInspectionForms1} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Selecting a Form Template when creating a new inspection.." />

and when adding a new **Inspection Form** to an existing **Inspection**.

<img src={ImageInspectionForms2} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Adding a Form Template to an existing Inspection." />

:::tip

If we were to put it into simpler terms:

- Think of a **Form Template** as a physical document you *could* bring with you to any inspection.
- If you decide to bring a copy of one of those documents with you to an inspection it is now an **Inspection Form** for that particular job.

:::

## Many Inspection Forms to one Inspection

Each **Inspection** can have one or more [**Inspection Forms**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms) associated with it. **Inspection Forms** allow you to associate different types of inspections with a single inspection site visit.

- e.g., If you are certified to perform multiple types of inspections (e.g. home inspection, asbestos inspection, radon inspection, etc.) on a property then you can pre-setup various [**Form Templates**](/docs/concepts/form-templates) and load them into the same **Inspection**.

    <img src={ImageInspectionForms3} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Multiple inspection Forms example." />


Each **Inspection Form** allows you to generate a distinct PDF report pertaining specifically to the inspection performed.

## Inspection Form framework

This is the data structure of your Inspection Form:

- **Category** — A high-level system (e.g. Interior, Roofing)
- **Component** — A specific element within a system (e.g. Flooring, Gutters)
- **Entry** — A specific observation (e.g. Water damage)
    - Each entry is a separate item in the report, so you can record multiple entries for the same component.
        - e.g., During your inspection you may be inspecting multiple exterior doors. If both the front door and the back door are damaged in *different* ways you could create separate entries for the exterior door component.
- **Field** — A specific input area (e.g. Description, Condition, Risk, Location, Recommended Action)
- **Field Option** — Predefined choices within a field (e.g. “Minor”, “Major”, “Not Present”)

<img src={ImageInspectionFormFramework} style={{ maxWidth: '900px', width: '100%' }} width={971} height={731} alt="Inspection Form framework diagram." />

This structure helps keep your reports organized and easy to navigate.

:::info

Every part of the framework is customizable. Here are some example use cases:

- If a **category** is unavailable by default, [you can create it](/docs/concepts/customizing-form-templates#creating-categories).
- If you're a roofing contractor and never inspect HVAC systems, you can delete the HVAC category.
- If the **component** you are inspecting has different areas (living room flooring vs. kitchen flooring) in different conditions then you can create multiple **entries**.
- If you're an Eneris admin at your company and need to edit the **field options** to use company approved terminology, you can do so quickly and without needing to interact with the Eneris team (but we're always happy to help).

:::

#### Shared Fields

**Shared Fields** automatically appear in every component of your [**Form Template**](/docs/concepts/form-templates). Use them for any common inputs in your inspection workflow.

- e.g. You may want a `Risk` or `Location` field available in every component. Creating it as a **Shared Field** means you only need to create them once and they will be available in every component.

This helps you save time and keeps your workflow and data entry consistent.

:::info

You can delete a **Shared Field** from a component if it's not required. However, note that you will not be able to re-add it at this time.

The `Status`, `Photos & Videos`, `Condition`, `Recommended Action`, and `Summary Label` fields cannot be deleted. [They can only be hidden](/docs/concepts/customizing-form-templates#field-visibility).

:::

### Photos and videos

Attach visual documentation to your **Inspection Forms** using media fields. Photos and videos help illustrate your findings and provide clear evidence for your clients.

You can add them from your media library or capture new ones directly in the Eneris app.

#### Supported file types

The media field supports the following file formats:

- **Images** – JPEG (.jpeg, .jpg), PNG (.png), HEIC (.heic), HEIF (.heif)
- **Videos** – MP4 (.mp4), MOV (.mov)

These formats ensure your media displays correctly in the final report.

#### Camera controls

The in-app camera includes familiar controls to help you capture clear, well-framed media:

- **Photo and video modes**: Switch between photo and video capture using the mode button next to the shutter. In video mode, tap the shutter to start recording and tap the stop button to finish the clip.
- **Zoom**: Pinch to zoom, or tap the zoom presets (e.g. 0.5x, 1x) to switch lenses instantly. Available presets depend on your device's cameras.
- **Aspect ratio**: Cycle between 4:3, 1:1, and 16:9 framing. Your selection is remembered for future captures. (1:1 is available for photos only.)
- **Flash**: Tap the bolt to cycle the capture flash through **auto**, **off**, and **on**. Auto is the default and fires only when the scene is dim, which keeps photos sharp in poorly lit spaces without you having to think about it. Your choice is remembered for future captures. The button is hidden on devices with no flash.
- **Torch**: Toggle a continuous light for dark spaces like attics and crawl spaces. While the torch is lit, the flash is held off, since firing it would briefly cut the torch light you turned on.
- **Focus**: Tap anywhere on the preview to focus on that spot.
- **Exposure**: After tapping to focus, drag the brightness slider to lighten or darken the shot. Available on supported devices.
- **Volume button capture**: Press either hardware volume button to take a photo, or to start and stop recording in video mode, just like your device's native camera.
- **Swipe to close**: Drag the camera preview downward to dismiss the camera. To avoid losing footage, swiping is disabled while a video is recording.

:::info

Videos are limited to **5 minutes** each. This applies to both videos recorded in the app and videos uploaded from your media library. While recording, a timer above the shutter shows your elapsed time against the limit, and recording stops automatically when you reach it. A longer video selected from your library is rejected with a **File rejected** message, so trim it or split it into multiple clips first.

:::

#### Camera permissions

The camera requires access to your device's **Camera**, **Microphone**, and **Media library**. If any of these permissions are missing, a **Permissions required** screen appears listing what still needs to be allowed. Select `Grant Permissions` to open your device settings, or `Return` to go back.

If your device has no available camera (or another app is using it), a **Camera unavailable** screen is shown instead.

#### Photo markup

Add visual annotations directly to your inspection photos to highlight specific areas of concern or provide additional context for your clients.

The photo markup feature helps you:

- **Draw attention to defects** – Circle cracks, highlight water damage, or point out specific issues.
- **Add clarity** – Use arrows, shapes, and drawing to make your findings unmistakable.
- **Professional presentation** – Marked-up photos appear in your final PDF report with annotations intact.

:::tip

Photo markup helps your clients quickly understand exactly what you're pointing out in each photo — reducing confusion and follow-up questions.

:::

##### Accessing photo markup

To add annotations to a photo:

1. Navigate to the component where you want to add an annotated photo.
2. Locate the media field.
3. Take a new photo or select an existing photo from your device.
4. Once the photo appears, select it to open the photo markup interface.
5. Use the annotation tools to mark up the photo.
6. Select `Save` to apply your annotations.

:::info

Photo markup replaces the original photo with the annotated version. Make sure your annotations are accurate before saving, as the original unmarked photo will not be preserved.

:::

##### Available annotation tools

The photo markup interface provides several tools to help you annotate photos effectively:

- **Pen tool** – Draw freehand lines to circle areas, underline text, or sketch annotations.
- **Shapes** – Add pre-defined shapes to highlight specific areas:
  - Arrow – Point to specific defects or areas of concern.
  - Circle – Highlight round areas or encircle multiple items.
  - Rectangle – Box in rectangular areas or sections.
  - Oval – Highlight oval-shaped areas.
  - Star – Mark priority items or call attention to critical findings.
- **Color picker** – Choose from multiple colors to differentiate between different types of issues or to ensure annotations are visible against the photo background.
- **Line width** – Adjust the thickness of your pen strokes and shape outlines for better visibility.
- **Photo rotation** – Rotate photos to correct their orientation, ensuring images appear upright and properly aligned in your final report.
- **Undo** – Revert your last annotation action, allowing you to correct mistakes one step at a time.
- **Clear** – Remove all annotations from the photo at once, letting you start fresh if needed.

<img src={ImageInspectionForms19} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Using photo markup tools to annotate inspection photos." />

:::tip

Use contrasting colors for your annotations to ensure they stand out clearly against the photo background.

:::

### Appendices

**Appendices** are supporting documents attached to an **Inspection Form**. Each one becomes a lettered appendix (A, B, C, and so on) at the end of the published report.

Use them for the evidence that belongs with a report but doesn't fit inside a category: an equipment certificate, a code excerpt backing up a deficiency, or a lab result for the property.

<img src={ImageAppendices1} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Appendices section on an Inspection Form listing three attached documents." />

A form can hold up to **5 appendices**.

#### Two ways to attach a file

Select **Add new** (or the pencil icon if files are already attached) to open the **Appendices** panel. From there you have two options.

<img src={ImageAppendices2} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Appendices panel showing three attached files and the two ways to add more." />

**Add reference file** attaches a document from your team's [**Reference Files**](/docs/concepts/reference-files) library. Search for the file, select it, and select **Done**. You can select several at once.

<img src={ImageAppendices3} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Attach from Reference Files picker with two documents selected." />

**Upload file** attaches a document to this form only. It is not added to the team library, which makes it the right choice for anything specific to a single property.

<img src={ImageAppendices4} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Upload file panel for attaching a document to this form only." />

Files attached from the library are marked with a **Reference file** tag so you can tell the two apart at a glance. Select the **✕** next to any file to remove it from the form.

:::info

Removing an appendix from a form never deletes the underlying document from your [**Reference Files**](/docs/concepts/reference-files) library.

:::

#### Appendices in the report

Appendices appear at the very end of the report, after every inspection category, in the order they are listed on the form.

In the **PDF report**, a final **Appendices** page lists each document as `Appendix A - <name>`, and the documents themselves follow as full pages.

In the **web report**, appendices get their own section and navigation tab, with each document lettered and linked.

<img src={ImageAppendices5} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Appendices section of a web report with documents lettered A, B, and C." />

:::info

Appendices are included in the main PDF report only. [**Supplementary forms**](/docs/concepts/form-templates#supplementary-forms) such as 4-Point and Wind Mitigation are published as their own documents and do not carry appendices.

:::

### Delete form

Deletes the **Inspection Form** you are currently in.

- You can always add new **Inspection Forms** in the **Inspection** page.

:::warning

Deleting an **Inspection Form** also deletes its associated [**Service Agreement**](/docs/concepts/service-agreement-templates#service-agreements-in-inspections), including any collected signatures, as well as any [published reports](/docs/concepts/reports#publishing-a-report) for that form. This action cannot be undone.

:::
