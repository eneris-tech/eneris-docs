# Saving

Eneris **automatically saves your inspection data locally to your device** as you inspect. This ensures that your progress is preserved even if you’re working offline or lose internet temporarily.

- Saved inspection data on your mobile device is known as an **Offline Form** [link to [offline form](https://www.notion.so/Offline-Forms-226d9f0aeb5a80b1aceac29afa949c64?pvs=21)]
- See **Syncing** [link to [Syncing](https://www.notion.so/Syncing-22cd9f0aeb5a80c7846ce549d62c1183?pvs=21)] for documentation on how to ensure your inspection data is **backed up online**.

:::info

For Eneris to save inspection data to your mobile device it must have sufficient free storage space.

We’ve taken steps to:

1. Minimize the amount of storage space Eneris needs.
2. Optimize how long unused saved reports remain on your device.

:::

## Auto-saving

Eneris automatically saves your inspection between **every thirty seconds to two minutes** as you work.

You can also save manually:

1. By navigating to the main **Inspection** page [link to [Inspection](https://www.notion.so/Inspections-226d9f0aeb5a8068b23fe5f466d0b8a8?pvs=21)] and selecting the `Save` button in the upper right.

<img src={ImageSaving1} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="Manual save button on the main Inspection page." />

1. By navigating to the `General` page of an **Inspection Form** [link to [Inspection Forms](https://www.notion.so/Inspection-Forms-22cd9f0aeb5a80f9906ce9df5b4cea45?pvs=21)] and selecting the `Save` button in the upper right.

<img src={ImageSaving2} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="Manual save button on the General page of an Inspection Form." />

## Saving vs. Syncing

While saving stores your work locally on your mobile device, **syncing** uploads the report to the cloud for backup and access across devices.

| **Action** | **Where it’s stored** | **When it happens** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Saving** | On your local mobile device | Every 30 seconds or manually. |
| **Syncing** | In the Eneris cloud | When you publish or share a report. You may also manually sync the report. |
