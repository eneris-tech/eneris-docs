# SOP Templates

**SOP Templates** (Standards of Practice Templates) allow you to attach professional standards documentation to your inspection reports. By linking an SOP to your [**Form Templates**](/docs/concepts/form-templates), you can ensure every inspection report includes the relevant standards your work adheres to.

SOPs are critical for:

- **Professional credibility** — Demonstrating adherence to industry standards builds trust with clients.
- **Compliance** — Many inspection associations require members to follow specific standards.
- **Consistency** — Ensuring all inspections reference the same standards across your team.

:::info

SOP Templates are reusable across multiple [**Form Templates**](/docs/concepts/form-templates). Set them up once and apply them to any template you create.

:::

## What is a Standard of Practice (SOP)?

A **Standard of Practice (SOP)** is a document published by professional inspection associations that defines the minimum requirements for conducting inspections. These standards outline what inspectors should and should not inspect, limitations, and the scope of the inspection.

Common examples include:

- **ASHI** — American Society of Home Inspectors
- **InterNACHI** — International Association of Certified Home Inspectors
- **CAHPI** — Canadian Association of Home & Property Inspectors
- **HIABC** — Home Inspectors Association BC

Including the SOP in your reports helps clients understand what was covered and sets appropriate expectations for the inspection scope.

## Managing SOP Templates

### View SOP Templates

To view your existing **SOP Templates**:

1. Select the hamburger menu in the upper left within the app on your mobile device.
2. Select `SOP templates`.

*[Screenshot: SOP Templates menu in sidebar]*

You will see a list of all available SOP Templates, including the default templates and any custom templates you have created.

*[Screenshot: List of SOP templates]*

### Create an SOP Template

To create a new **SOP Template**:

1. Select the hamburger menu in the upper left within the app on your mobile device.
2. Select `SOP templates`.
3. Select `Create new`.
4. Enter a **Name** for your template (this is for internal reference only).
5. Enter a **Title** (this appears in your PDF reports, defaults to "Standards of Practice").
6. Optionally, enter a **URL** linking to the full SOP document online.
7. Optionally, enter the **Body** content — this supports markdown formatting for headers, lists, and emphasis.
8. Select `Save`.

*[Screenshot: Create new SOP template form]*

:::info

The **Name** field is used to identify the template in your list. The **Title** field is what appears in your client-facing PDF reports.

:::

### Edit an SOP Template

To modify an existing **SOP Template**:

1. Navigate to `SOP templates` from the hamburger menu.
2. Select the template you wish to edit.
3. Update the **Name**, **Title**, **URL**, or **Body** fields as needed.
4. Select `Save`.

*[Screenshot: Edit SOP template showing title, URL, and body fields]*

:::info

Changes to an **SOP Template** only affect new [**Inspections**](/docs/concepts/inspections) created after the change. Existing inspections retain the SOP content that was embedded when they were created.

:::

### Delete an SOP Template

To delete an **SOP Template**:

1. Navigate to `SOP templates` from the hamburger menu.
2. Select the template you wish to delete.
3. Scroll to the bottom and select `Delete`.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

:::warning

Deleting an SOP Template cannot be undone. Existing inspections that use this template will retain their embedded SOP content.

:::

## Linking SOP to Form Templates

To associate an **SOP Template** with a [**Form Template**](/docs/concepts/form-templates):

1. Navigate to `Form templates` from the hamburger menu.
2. Select the **Form Template** you wish to modify.
3. Locate the **SOP Template** field.
4. Select the desired **SOP Template** from the dropdown.
5. Save your changes.

*[Screenshot: Linking SOP template to a Form Template]*

:::info

Once linked, the SOP is automatically embedded when creating new [**Inspections**](/docs/concepts/inspections) using that **Form Template**. The SOP content (title, URL, and body) is copied into the inspection, allowing for per-inspection modifications if needed.

:::

## SOP in Inspection Forms

When you create an [**Inspection**](/docs/concepts/inspections) using a **Form Template** that has an SOP linked, the SOP content is automatically embedded into the [**Inspection Form**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms).

You can view and modify the embedded SOP content directly within the inspection if you need to make ad-hoc changes for a specific job. This is useful when:

- A client requests additional scope clarifications
- You need to add project-specific limitations
- You want to reference a different version of the standards

Changes made to the SOP within an **Inspection Form** only affect that specific inspection — they do not update the original **SOP Template**.

## SOP in Reports

When you [publish a report](/docs/concepts/reports#publishing-a-report), the SOP content appears in your PDF output. The SOP section includes:

- **Title** — The display title (e.g., "Standards of Practice")
- **URL** — A link to the full SOP document (if provided)
- **Body** — The SOP content with any markdown formatting rendered

*[Screenshot: SOP section in PDF report]*

:::tip

Including your SOP in reports demonstrates compliance with professional standards and helps set clear expectations with your clients about the scope and limitations of the inspection.

:::
