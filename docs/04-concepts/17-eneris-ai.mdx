# Eneris AI

**Eneris AI** is an assistant that writes your observations into the **Inspection Form** while you inspect. Type or dictate a note, or send a photo, and Eneris AI drafts the change for you: the right component, the right fields, and the wording in your style.

It can also proofread your comments, check them for consistency, and answer questions about what the form already records.

**You stay in control.** Eneris AI never edits your form directly. Everything it drafts arrives as a **suggestion**, and nothing changes on the form until you accept it.

:::info

Eneris AI is in **Beta**. It is available to every team, but it stays off on each device until you turn it on.

:::

## Turning on Eneris AI

Eneris AI is a [**Device preference**](/docs/account/preferences#device-preferences), so you turn it on separately on each phone, tablet, and computer you use. Each web browser counts as its own device.

1. Select **Preferences** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Device preferences**, set **Eneris AI** to `Enabled`.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiEnable} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="The Preferences page with the Eneris AI device preference set to Enabled and the new Eneris AI section below it." />

Once it is enabled, an **Eneris AI** section appears on the **Preferences** page with your [**AI instructions**](#ai-instructions) and [**Saved messages**](#saved-messages).

Set it back to `Disabled` at any time to hide Eneris AI on that device. Your chats and suggestions are kept, and they are waiting for you when you turn it on again.

## Where to find Eneris AI

Eneris AI works inside **Inspection Forms** only. It does not appear while you edit a [**Form Template**](/docs/concepts/form-templates).

Open any **Inspection Form** and you'll find Eneris AI in four places:

- **Form Tab Bar**: **Eneris AI** is the first item in the [**Form Tab Bar**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms#form-tab-bar). Select it to open or close the Eneris AI panel.
- **Component cards**: the AI button at the left of every component opens [the notes for that component](#notes-on-a-component-or-section).
- **Categories**: the button labelled with the category name, at the top of a category's components, does the same for the whole category.
- **Form sections**: an AI button sits beside the **General limitations**, **General remarks**, **Property details**, and **Internal memo** headings, and beside each category's **Limitations**.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiEntryPoints} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="The Plumbing category of an Inspection Form with the Plumbing category button, a component card's AI button, and Eneris AI in the Form Tab Bar highlighted." />

A dot on an AI button tells you what is going on:

- A **pulsing dot** on **Eneris AI** in the tab bar means Eneris AI is working.
- An **orange dot** means Eneris AI has a [question](#answering-questions) for you.
- A **steady dot** means suggestions are waiting for your review. On a component or category, it means that part of the form has suggestions waiting.

## The Eneris AI panel

The panel is where you talk to Eneris AI and review what it suggests.

On a desktop computer, the panel docks to the right of the form so you can work on both at once. Drag its edge to make it wider or narrower; Eneris remembers the width on that device. The [**Media folder panel**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms#media-folder-panel) can stay open beside it when the window is wide enough.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiPanel} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="The Eneris AI panel docked beside an Inspection Form, showing an observation, its work log, and a Review 1 suggestion link." />

On phones and tablets, the panel opens over the form instead. Select **Eneris AI** in the tab bar again to close it and return to the form.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiPanelIos} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} width={1206} height={2355} alt="The Eneris AI panel open over an Inspection Form on iPhone." />

The panel has two views. Switch between them with the button at the top right:

- **Chat**: your conversation with Eneris AI.
- **Suggestions**: every suggestion Eneris AI has drafted on this form, ready for you to [review](#reviewing-suggestions).

### Chats

You can keep several chats on a form. Using a separate chat for each part of the job, such as the roof or the crawlspace, keeps each conversation focused, and more than one chat can be working at the same time.

Select the chat name at the top of the panel to switch chats, start a `New chat`, or delete one. A new chat takes its name from your first message.

Your chats are private to you, and each one belongs to a single **Inspection Form**. Whichever chat a suggestion came from, it shows up in the one **Suggestions** view.

### Writing a message

Type in the message box at the bottom of the panel. A message can be an observation, an instruction, or a question:

- **Observation**: "Water heater has no drip pan and the TPR discharge line ends 2 ft above the floor."
- **Instruction**: "Add critical findings to the general remarks."
- **Question**: "List all the electrical issues."

A new chat offers a few starter messages to get you going. Select one to send it.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiComposer} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="A new chat with starter messages, and the message box with the Model, Effort, microphone, photo, saved messages, and Auto-accept controls highlighted." />

The buttons under the message box add more than text:

- **Microphone**: record a voice note of up to 2 minutes. Eneris AI turns it into text and places it in the message box so you can check it before you send. Nothing is sent by itself, and the audio is never stored.
- **Camera** (mobile app only): take a photo or video and attach it to the message.
- **Photo album**: attach photos or videos from your device.
- **Bookmark**: insert one of your [saved messages](#saved-messages).

On a desktop computer, you can also drag photos and videos onto the message box from your computer, or from the [**Media folder panel**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms#media-folder-panel).

:::tip

Eneris AI can read a lot from a photo. Send a picture of an appliance's data plate and it can work out the age of the unit from its serial number.

:::

### Model and effort

**Model** chooses which AI model drafts your suggestions. The recommended model suits most inspections. Others can be more capable on harder requests, but they use more [AI credits](#ai-credits). Each model in the list shows how it compares.

**Effort** sets how much Eneris AI thinks before it answers. `Medium` is recommended. `High` gives more careful work, but takes longer and uses more credits. `Low` is quicker and uses fewer.

The model and effort you choose become your defaults for new chats on that device.

### Runs and the work log

Sending a message starts a **run**: Eneris AI reads your message, looks at the form, and drafts suggestions. While it works, a **Work log** card in the chat shows each step as it happens. Select a finished work log to see what Eneris AI did and why.

When the run finishes, Eneris AI replies with a short summary. If it drafted suggestions, select the **Review** link under the reply, such as **Review 2 suggestions**, to go straight to them.

- **Send another message while it works**: your new message joins the run in progress, so you don't need to wait.
- **Stop a run**: while a run is working and the message box is empty, the send button becomes **Stop**. Suggestions already drafted are kept.

:::warning

Eneris AI reads the form from the device that started the run. Keep Eneris open until the run finishes. If you close the app, the run ends early with **Device went away**.

:::

### Answering questions

When Eneris AI needs something only you know, it asks. The question is pinned above the message box.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiQuestion} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="A question from Eneris AI pinned above the message box, with quick replies and an answer field." />

Select one of the quick replies, or type or dictate your own answer. If you'd rather not answer, select the **X** and Eneris AI goes ahead with its best guess.

## Reviewing suggestions

Select **Suggestions** at the top of the panel to see every suggestion on the form. Pending suggestions are shown first; select the filter button to show accepted or rejected suggestions too, or to narrow the list to one category.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiSuggestions} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="The Suggestions view with a pending Water Heater suggestion showing its changes, and the Accept, Reject, and Revise controls highlighted." />

Each suggestion card names the component it changes and shows exactly what will change. New text is tinted, and anything that will be removed is struck through.

On each card you can:

- **Accept** the suggestion to write it into the form.
- **Reject** it to leave the form as it is.
- **Revise** it: type what you'd like changed in the revision box, such as "mention the corrosion too", and select `Revise`. Eneris AI redrafts the suggestion for you.
- Select the **map pin** to open the component on the form.
- Select the **light bulb** to see why Eneris AI suggested it.
- Select the **speech bubble** to see where the suggestion came from, such as your message or a photo, and `Show in chat` to jump to it.

Select `Accept all pending` to accept every pending suggestion at once.

When you accept, the changed fields briefly highlight on the form. If you later edit one of those fields yourself, it is marked **Edited by hand**.

:::warning

If someone edits the component after Eneris AI drafted its suggestion, Eneris shows you how the entry reads now before you accept, so you don't overwrite their change by accident.

:::

### Editing a suggestion before you accept

Select the **pencil** on a suggestion card to open **Edit suggestion**. It shows the whole entry the way it will read once you accept, with the suggested changes marked, and you can adjust any field before saving.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiEditSuggestion} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Edit suggestion showing the suggested approximate age selected, the old value struck through, and the suggested recommended action tinted." />

From **Edit suggestion** you can also:

- Select `Save as new entry` to add the suggestion as a separate entry instead of changing the existing one.
- Select the **pencil** beside the component name to move the suggestion to a different category or component.

Select `Save` to keep your changes. The suggestion stays pending until you accept it.

### Undoing an accepted suggestion

Changed your mind? Select `Undo` on an accepted suggestion. Eneris puts back what was there before and returns the suggestion to **Pending**. Any edits you made by hand after accepting are kept, and a component or category the suggestion created is removed.

### Auto-accept

Turn on **Auto-accept** under the message box when you trust Eneris AI with a chat. Suggestions drafted while it is on go straight into the form, and you can still review them under **Suggestions** and `Undo` any you don't want.

Auto-accept applies to one chat at a time, and changes to [AI instructions](#ai-instructions) always wait for you to accept them.

:::warning

With **Auto-accept** on, Eneris AI changes your form without asking. Review the changes before you publish the report.

:::

### What Eneris AI can change

Eneris AI can suggest changes to:

- Component fields, including adding a new entry to a component
- New components and new categories
- A category's **Limitations**, and the **General limitations**
- **General remarks**, such as weather conditions, attendees, and overall observations
- **Property details**, such as year built, building size, and property features
- The **Internal memo**
- Photos you send it: where they go, and their captions

When accepting a suggestion adds a value that isn't yet one of a field's options, the value is added to that field's options too.

## Notes on a component or section

Select the AI button on a component card, a category, or a form section to open its notes. You'll see the suggestions waiting for that part of the form, and a message box for comments about it.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiComponentSheet} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="The Water Heater notes dialog with a pending suggestion and the Add comments about water heater message box." />

Messages sent from here go to your current chat, already tagged with the component or section, so you don't have to tell Eneris AI where the observation belongs.

## AI instructions

**AI instructions** teach Eneris AI how you write: the terms you prefer, how long notes should be, and phrases to always or never use. Eneris AI follows them every time it drafts a suggestion.

There are three sets of instructions:

| Set | Who it applies to | Where to edit it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Form-specific instructions** | This **Inspection Form** only | The Eneris AI panel |
| **My instructions** | Only you, on every form | **Preferences** or the Eneris AI panel |
| **Shared instructions** | Everyone on your team, on every form | **Preferences** or the Eneris AI panel |

When two sets disagree, the more specific one wins: form-specific instructions first, then yours, then your team's. Anything you say in a chat message overrides all three for that request.

To edit your instructions, select **Preferences**, then `AI instructions` under **Eneris AI**. Write your instructions and select `Save my instructions` or `Save shared instructions`. In the Eneris AI panel, select **AI instructions** at the top of the chat to edit all three sets, including the instructions for the form you have open.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiInstructions} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="The AI instructions page with four example rules in My instructions and an empty Shared instructions card." />

:::tip

Write one rule per line, the way you'd brief a new inspector:

- **Be specific**: "Write crawlspace as one word", not "Fix spelling".
- **Say what to do**, not only what to avoid.
- **Quote the wording** you want when it matters.
- **Keep rules short**: split a paragraph into separate lines.

:::

:::info

Anyone on your team can edit **Shared instructions**, and changes apply to everyone's next draft.

:::

### Setting up Eneris AI from your forms

Not sure where to start? Eneris AI can write your first **Shared instructions** for you by reading forms you've already completed.

When no instructions have been written yet, a `Set up Eneris AI` button appears in the chat. You can also select `Set up from forms` in the **AI instructions** sheet at any time.

1. Select `Set up Eneris AI`.
2. Choose which recent inspection forms Eneris AI should read. The newest are selected for you.
3. Select `Read 8 forms and suggest shared instructions`. The number matches how many forms you chose.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiSetup} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="The Set up Eneris AI dialog with seven recent inspection forms selected and the Read 7 forms and suggest shared instructions button." />

Eneris AI opens a **Set up Eneris AI** chat, reads the comments in those forms, and proposes **Shared instructions** that capture how your team writes. Review them like any other suggestion: `Accept`, `Accept and edit`, `Reject`, or ask for a change.

## Saved messages

**Saved messages** are messages you send often, saved so you don't have to retype them. Select the **bookmark** under the message box and choose one to place it in the message box.

To save a message you sent in a chat, select it and choose **Save this message to reuse later**.

To manage your saved messages, select **Preferences**, then `Saved messages` under **Eneris AI**.

<img src={ImageEnerisAiSavedMessages} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="The Saved messages page listing a shared End-of-day proofread message and a private Water heater checklist message." />

To add one, select `Create new message`, enter a **Name** and the **Message**, and choose who can see it:

- **Private**: only you can see and use it.
- **Shared with team**: everyone on your team can see, use, and edit it.

## Working offline

On the iOS and Android apps, you can keep talking to Eneris AI without a connection. Your messages, photos, and answers are saved on the device and marked **queued, pending sync**.

When you're back online:

1. [**Sync**](/docs/concepts/syncing) the form. This delivers your queued messages to Eneris AI. Background syncing delivers them for forms you don't have open.
2. Select `Run` in the bar above the message box to have Eneris AI work through them.

Syncing delivers your messages but never starts a run by itself, so Eneris AI doesn't spend your credits until you say so.

While offline:

- Suggestions and work logs are hidden until you reconnect.
- Voice notes are unavailable, so type your note instead.
- Your own and shared instructions, and saved messages, can be used but not changed. Form-specific instructions can still be edited.

:::info

In a web browser, Eneris AI needs a connection to send messages. If the connection drops, Eneris reconnects automatically.

:::

## AI credits

Eneris AI usage is measured in **AI credits**. Each run uses credits based on the model and effort you choose, and voice notes use a small amount for transcription.

| Plan | Monthly AI credits |
| --- | --- |
| Paid plans | 2,000 for each team member |
| Free trial | 2,000 shared by the team |
| Free plan | 200 shared by the team |

Credits reset each month. To see how many you've used, open **Settings** and look under [**Subscription details**](/docs/account/settings#subscription-details).

If you run out, Eneris AI tells you **Usage limit reached** and can't take new messages until your credits reset next month. Sending a lot of requests in a short time can also pause Eneris AI briefly; try again in a moment.

## Reporting a bad response

If Eneris AI gets something wrong, select its reply, then the **thumbs down**. You can choose what went wrong, such as **Inaccurate** or **Wrong component**, and add details. Your feedback goes to the Eneris team so we can improve Eneris AI.
