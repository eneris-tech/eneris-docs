# Service Agreement Templates

**Service Agreements** allow you to create, customize, and enforce multi-party service agreements directly within Eneris. By requiring clients to sign agreements before inspections, you establish liability protection and ensure all parties understand the scope and terms of your services.

Service Agreements are critical for:

- **Liability protection** — Ensure agreements are signed before performing inspections.
- **Multi-signature support** — Handle unmarried buyers, siblings, investors, and other multi-party scenarios.
- **Workflow efficiency** — Eliminate the need for external tools e-signature tools or paper-based agreements.

:::info

Service Agreement Templates are reusable across multiple [**Form Templates**](/docs/concepts/form-templates). Set them up once and apply them to any template you create.

:::

## What is a Service Agreement?

A **Service Agreement** is a legally binding document that defines the terms and conditions of your inspection services. It outlines what you will and will not inspect, your liability limitations, and other important terms that protect both you and your clients.

Including a signed Service Agreement with your inspections helps:

- Protect you from liability claims
- Set clear expectations about the scope of the inspection
- Provide documentation that clients acknowledged and agreed to your terms

## Default Service Agreement Templates

The following default **Service Agreement Templates** are available in Eneris:

- Eneris template
- International Association of Certified Home Inspectors (InterNACHI) service agreement template
- Canadian Association of Home & Property Inspectors (CAHPI) service agreement template
- Home Inspectors Association BC (HIABC) service agreement template

:::info

If there is a template you would like to see, [we would love to hear about it](/docs/support/contact-support).

## Managing Service Agreement Templates

### View Service Agreement Templates

To view your existing **Service Agreement Templates**:

1. Navigate to **Agreement templates** in the sidebar.

<img src={ImageServiceAgreements1} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Service Agreement Templates menu in sidebar." />

You will see a list of all available Service Agreement Templates, including any templates you have created.

<img src={ImageServiceAgreements2} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="List of Service Agreement Templates." />

### Create a Service Agreement Template

To create a new **Service Agreement Template**:

1. Navigate to **Agreement templates** in the sidebar.
2. Select `Create new agreement template`.
3. Choose from one of the available templates or create a blank template to start from scratch.
4. Update the `Agreement template name` as necessary and then select `Create new`.
5. Enter a **Name** for your template (this is for internal reference only).
6. Enter a **Title** (this appears in your PDF reports).
7. Enter the agreement content in the **Body** field.
8. Optionally, insert [**Dynamic Tags**](/docs/concepts/service-agreement-templates#dynamic-tags) to dynamically populate inspection-specific information.
9. Select `Save changes` at the bottom of the page.

<img src={ImageServiceAgreements3} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Create new Service Agreement Template." />

:::info

The **Name** field is used to identify the template in your list. The **Title** field is what appears in your client-facing PDF reports.

:::

### Edit a Service Agreement Template

To modify an existing **Service Agreement Template**:

1. Navigate to **Agreement templates** in the sidebar.
2. Select the template you wish to edit.
3. Update the **Name**, **Title**, and **Body** fields as needed.
4. Select `Save changes`.

:::info

Changes to a **Service Agreement Template** only affect new [**Inspections**](/docs/concepts/inspections) created after the change. Existing inspections retain the Service Agreement content that was embedded when they were created.

:::

<img src={ImageServiceAgreements4} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Edit a Service Agreement Template." />

### Delete a Service Agreement Template

To delete a **Service Agreement Template**:

1. Navigate to **Agreement templates** in the sidebar.
2. Select the template you wish to delete.
3. Scroll to the bottom and select `Delete agreement template`.
5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

:::warning

Deleting a Service Agreement Template cannot be undone. Existing inspections that use this template will retain their embedded Service Agreement content.

:::

## Dynamic Tags

**Dynamic Tags** allow you to dynamically insert inspection-specific information into your Service Agreement Templates. When a service agreement is [`Published`](/docs/concepts/service-agreement-templates#service-agreement-states), these tags are automatically replaced with the actual values.

Insert them using the tag buttons beneath the body field, which are grouped into **Contacts**, **This inspection**, and **Your business**.

**Contacts**

| Tag | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `{{CLIENT}}` | The name of the inspection's primary contact |
| `{{SECONDARY_CLIENT}}` | The name of a second contact sharing the primary contact's role, such as a co-buyer |
| `{{CLIENT_ADDRESS}}` | The primary contact's own address |

**This inspection**

| Tag | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `{{ADDRESS}}` | The address of the property being inspected |
| `{{DATE}}` | The scheduled inspection date |
| `{{TIME}}` | The scheduled inspection time |
| `{{SUBTOTAL}}` | The invoice subtotal for the inspection |
| `{{TOTAL}}` | The invoice total for the inspection |

**Your business**

| Tag | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `{{INSPECTOR}}` | The inspector's name from their [**Inspector Profile**](/docs/concepts/inspector-profiles) |
| `{{INSPECTOR_EMAIL}}` | The inspector's email address |
| `{{BUSINESS_NAME}}` | Your business name |
| `{{BUSINESS_PHONE}}` | Your business phone number |
| `{{BUSINESS_WEBSITE}}` | Your business website |
| `{{BUSINESS_ADDRESS}}` | Your business address |

:::info

When a Service Agreement Template is applied to an Inpsection Form, Dynamic Tags are converted to actual text values. This conversion is one-way — the tags cannot be reverted once applied.

You can preview how the Dynamic Tags will appear before publishing the Service Agreement for client signature(s).

:::

## Inspector Signature

Your signature appears on all Service Agreements you send for signing. To set up your inspector signature:

1. Navigate to your [**Inspector Profile**](/docs/concepts/inspector-profiles).
2. Locate the **Inspector Signature** field.
3. Sign or select `Generate signature from name`
4. Select `Submit`
5. Select `Save changes`

Your signature will automatically appear on all Service Agreements associated with your inspections.

:::info

Your inspector signature is a one-time setup. Once configured, it applies to all Service Agreements across all inspections where you are the primary inspector.

:::

Once a signature is on file, an **Include signature in** checklist appears beneath the signature pad, letting you choose which documents it is stamped on: service agreements, report cover letters, and invoices. Service agreements are selected by default, so no action is needed to keep signing them as before. See [Inspector signature placement](/docs/concepts/inspector-profiles#inspector-signature-placement) if you want to change it.

6. Navigate back a page then select `Save` in the upper right.

## Service Agreements in Inspections

Service Agreements are managed from the **Service agreements** section on the [**Inspection**](/docs/concepts/inspections) page. Each agreement appears as a card showing the form it belongs to, the agreement title, its current state, and the list of signers.

When you create an [**Inspection**](/docs/concepts/inspections) using a **Form Template** that has a Service Agreement linked, the agreement is automatically created and appears in this section.

<img src={ImageServiceAgreements7} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="Service agreements section on the Inspection page showing an agreement card per form." />

### Adding a Service Agreement

To add a Service Agreement to an inspection:

1. Navigate to the **Inspection** page.
2. Locate the **Service agreements** section.
3. Select `Add`.
4. In the **New service agreement** sheet, select the **Inspection form** the agreement is for. The first form without an agreement is preselected, and forms that already have one are marked `(Already has an agreement)`.
5. Select the **Agreement template** to use.
6. Select `Create agreement`.

<img src={ImageServiceAgreements8} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={800} alt="New service agreement sheet with the inspection form and agreement template selections." />

The new agreement is created in the `Draft` state, so you can edit the agreement and signers before publishing.

:::info

Each [Inspection Form](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms) can have a maximum of one Service Agreement.

:::

### Making Ad-Hoc Changes

You can modify the Service Agreement content for a specific inspection by selecting the ellipsis on the agreement card and choosing `Edit agreement`. This is useful when:

- A client requests additional scope clarifications
- You need to add project-specific limitations
- You want to customize terms for a particular situation

Changes made to an inspection's Service Agreement only affect that specific inspection. They do not update the original **Service Agreement Template**.

:::warning

Making changes to a Service Agreement after it has been signed will clear all existing signatures. Clients will need to re-sign the updated agreement.

:::

### Service Agreement States
Serivce Agreements can be in one of three states: `Draft`, `Published`, or `Signed`.

- `Draft` — Service Agreement can still be configured. Either the content is being edited or signers are being added.
- `Published` — Service Agreement has been finalized and is ready for signature. Add additional signers and/or request missing signatures.
- `Signed` — Service Agreement has been signed by all signers.

<img src={ImageServiceAgreementStates} style={{ maxWidth: '700px', width: '100%' }} width={571} height={159} alt="Service Agreement states." />

:::warning

Changes to the Service Agreement are possible regardless of what state it is in. However, making changes when it is in the `Published` or `Signed` states will clear all existing signatures. Clients will need to re-sign the updated agreement.

:::

### Publishing a Service Agreement
Publishing the Service Agreement means the agreement is finalized and ready for signature.

1. Navigate to the **Inspection** page.
2. Locate the agreement's card in the **Service agreements** section.
3. Select `Publish agreement`.
4. The Service Agreement is now in the `Published` state and ready for signatures.

:::info

The `Publish agreement` button is only enabled once the agreement has at least one signer. Add signers with the `Add signer` button on the agreement card.

:::

:::info

Once all signers have signed the Service Agreement it will automatically convert to the [`Signed`](/docs/concepts/service-agreement-templates#service-agreement-states) state. All signers will receive an email confirmation.

:::

### Viewing Signature Status

The **Signers** list on the agreement card displays the current signature status, which shows how many parties have signed (e.g., "2/3 signed"), with a `Signed` badge next to each signer who has completed their signature. This allows you to quickly see if any signatures are still pending.

## Multi-Signature Support

Eneris supports up to **10 signatures** on a single Service Agreement, accommodating complex scenarios such as:

- Unmarried couples purchasing together
- Siblings or family members as co-buyers
- Investors or business partners
- Any combination of multiple parties

### Configuring Signers

Signers are managed in the **Signers** list on the agreement card. Select `Add signer` and choose which [contacts](/docs/concepts/inspections#contacts) from the Inspection need to sign. Only contacts associated with the inspection can be added as signers.

Signatures are arranged in two columns in the final document to save space, with the inspector's signature appearing last.

:::info

At this time, only the primary inspector's signature will appear in the Service Agreement.

:::

### In-Person Signing

For in-person signing:

1. Ensure the Service Agreement is in the [`Published`](/docs/concepts/service-agreement-templates#service-agreement-states) state.
2. Locate the signer in the **Signers** list on the agreement card.
3. Select the `Sign` pill next to their name.
4. Hand the device to the signer.
5. The Service Agreement will appear with a name and signature box at the bottom of the page.
6. The signer types their name and signs/generates their signature.
7. Select `Submit`.

### Off-Site Signing

To send a Service Agreement to all pending signers at once:

1. Ensure the Service Agreement is in the [`Published`](/docs/concepts/service-agreement-templates#service-agreement-states) state.
2. Select `Request signatures` on the agreement card.
3. Optionally, choose an [**Email template**](/docs/concepts/email-templates) to fill in the subject and message for you.
4. Optionally, add a personal **Message** for the recipients.
5. Select `Send request`. This sends a signature request email to every signer who has not yet signed the Service Agreement.

The signature request email includes the inspection address, along with the inspection date and time shown in the signer's time zone.

:::info

The same **Email template** menu appears when you select `Share agreement PDF`, and when you request a signature from a single signer. See [**Email Templates**](/docs/concepts/email-templates#setting-a-default-template) to set one as the automatic choice.

:::

:::tip

To request a single signature instead, select the `Request` pill next to an individual signer's name.

:::

:::info

Signers without an email address on their contact are skipped when sending requests. Add an email to their contact first, using the ellipsis menu next to their name and selecting `Edit contact`.

:::

### Managing Signatures

If a signature needs to be corrected:

1. Locate the signer in the **Signers** list on the agreement card.
2. Select the ellipsis menu next to their name.
3. Select `Delete signature`.
4. Select the `Sign` or `Request` pill next to their name to collect a new signature.

:::info

Signatures are tied to the Service Agreement. If the agreement is deleted, all associated signatures are also removed.

:::

## Electronic Signing Interface

The signing interface works across all platforms:

**Web:**
- Sign using your mouse or trackpad
- Full signature pad interface

**Mobile:**
- Sign using your finger
- Optimized for touch screens

**Features:**
- **Cursive pre-filled option** — Generate a cursive signature based on the signer's name
- **Free-form drawing** — Draw a signature manually
- **Delete and re-do** — Clear and restart if you make a mistake

## Signature Notifications

### For Signers

After all signers successfully sign a Service Agreement, everyone will receive an email confirmation acknowledging their signature has been recorded.

### For Inspectors

When all required parties have signed the Service Agreement, you receive an email notification confirming the agreement is fully completed.

## Service Agreement in Reports

When you [publish a report](/docs/concepts/reports#publishing-a-report), the Service Agreement appears in the PDF report.

**Placement:**
- Second-to-last section (before Standards of Practice)
- Added to the Table of Contents

**Content includes:**
- **Title** — The Service Agreement title you specified
- **Body** — The full agreement text
- **Signatures** — All collected signatures with printed names and date of signature

## Standalone PDF

You can generate and share a fully signed Service Agreement as a standalone PDF document, separate from the inspection report.

### Generating a standalone Service Agreement PDF
To generate a standalone PDF:

1. Navigate to the **Inspection** page.
2. Locate the agreement's card in the **Service agreements** section.
3. Select `View PDF`.

This helps further verify the signatures have been completed.

### Sharing a standalone Service Agreement PDF
To share a standalone PDF:

1. Navigate to the **Inspection** page.
2. Locate the agreement's card in the **Service agreements** section.
3. Select the ellipsis on the card. This is the actions menu.
4. Select `Share agreement PDF`.

:::info

`Share agreement PDF` is available once the agreement is in the `Published` or `Signed` state.

:::

This is useful when clients request a copy of just the signed agreement without the full inspection report.
