# Syncing

Syncing ensures that your inspection data is uploaded to the cloud, so it’s backed up and accessible across all your devices.

Syncing sends the latest version of your **Inspection** [link to [Inspection](https://www.notion.so/Inspections-226d9f0aeb5a8068b23fe5f466d0b8a8?pvs=21)] from your local device to the Eneris cloud. This keeps your data safe, allows for collaboration, and enables seamless access to your **Inspection** from other devices.

## Auto-syncing

**Auto-sync** occurs when you **publish or preview a report**.

You can also **manually sync** by selecting the **Sync** option in the form properties.

<img src={ImageSyncing1} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="Manual sync button." />

## Syncing vs. Saving

While saving stores your work locally on your mobile device, **syncing** uploads the report to the cloud for backup and access across devices.

| Action | Where it’s stored | When it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saving | On your local mobile device | Every 2 minutes or manually |
| Syncing | In the Eneris cloud | When you publish or share a report. You may also manually sync the report. |
