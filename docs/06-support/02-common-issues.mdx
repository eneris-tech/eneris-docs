# Common issues

## Unable to login

If you are unable to login to your Eneris account select the `Forgot password?` option on the sign in screen.

- This sends you a verification code to your email to reset your password.
- What you enter as your **username** and in the **forgot password email** are **case-sensitive**. Ensure you enter them exactly as registered when logging in.

:::info

Eneris Elevate (our new application) and Eneris Legacy (our previous application) are **completely separate applications**. They **do not** share a login.

- To use Eneris Elevate you must create a new account.
    - If you had an Eneris Legacy account **you may use the same email** when creating an Eneris Elevate account.
    - If you are an existing Eneris Legacy user and would like to **migrate your account to Eneris Elevate** [see the instructions here](/docs/getting-started/migrating-your-account-from-Eneris-Legacy).

:::

## Logging in to Eneris Legacy

If you are trying to login to Eneris Legacy (our previous application):

- **Computer:** [Use this link](https://ener.is/login).
- **Apple device:** Ensure you have the [Eneris Legacy application downloaded from the App Store.](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/eneris/id1448765778)
- **Android device:** Ensure you have the [Eneris Legacy application downloaded from the Play Store.](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ca.eneris.ener.is&hl=en_CA)

## Assistance with a specific Inspection Form

If you need help with a specific [**Inspection Form**](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms) our support team may request that you submit an [error report](/docs/concepts/inspection-forms#submit-error-report).

:::info

Error reports can be submitted quickly and only requires a press of a button.

:::

## Photos not uploading on PC

If you are experiencing issues uploading photos on your PC, this may be due to the file format. Specifically, **HEIC (.heic)** and **HEIF (.heif)** image formats are not currently supported for upload on PC.

:::info

We are investigating and hope to have a fix soon. In the meantime, you may need to convert your images to a supported format such as JPEG (.jpg) or PNG (.png) before uploading.

:::
