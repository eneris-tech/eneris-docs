# Contact support

## General

Please email [support@eneris.ca](mailto:support@eneris.ca) or call our support number 1-888-500-0504.

## Support hours

Our support hours are Monday to Friday 8am to 5pm PST (**excluding Canadian holidays**).

:::info

If the phone lines are busy please leave a message with your **full name, phone number, and description of your support request** and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

:::

## Assistance with a specific Inspection Form

If you need help with a specific **Inspection Form** [link to [inspection forms](https://www.notion.so/Inspection-Forms-22cd9f0aeb5a80f9906ce9df5b4cea45?pvs=21)] our support team may request that you submit an error report [link to [error report](https://www.notion.so/Inspection-Forms-22cd9f0aeb5a80f9906ce9df5b4cea45?pvs=21)].

:::info

Error reports can be submitted quickly and only requires a press of a button.

:::
