# Contact support

## General

Please email [support@eneris.ca](mailto:support@eneris.ca) or call our support number [1-888-500-0504](tel:18885000504).

## Support hours

Our support hours are Monday to Friday 8am to 5pm PST (**excluding Canadian holidays**).

:::info

If the phone lines are busy please leave a message with your **full name, phone number, and description of your support request** and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

:::
