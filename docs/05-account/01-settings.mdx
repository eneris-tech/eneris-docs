# Settings

The **Settings** page allows you to update the name and email associated with your Eneris account. It also provides information on your subscription details and a few other details.

The **Settings** page can be found on the home screen.

<img src={ImageSettings1} style={{ maxWidth: '250px', width: '100%' }} alt="Settings menu." />

## Name

Select the edit icon to update your name.

## Email

Select the edit icon to update your email.

- A verification email will be sent to the email address you enter.

## Subscription details

Details on your current subscription plan.

## System preferences

### Theme

Allows you to set the display theme of the Eneris application.

- For the best experience, we recommend leaving the setting on `System`. This automatically matches the display settings of your device.

### App details

Displays the Eneris application version, build number, and patch ID.

### Import account

Allows you to import your Eneris Legacy account. For more information, see Migrating your account from Eneris Legacy. [link to migrating account]

## Log out

Logs you out of your current Eneris account.

## Delete my account

Allows you to delete your Eneris account.

What will be permanently deleted:

- All your inspection reports and forms.
- Your profile and business information.
- All uploaded photos and documents.
- Your team and member data.
- All templates and customizations.
- Personal information tied to your billing.
- Account login credentials.

:::info

Account deletions requests may take up to 14 days to fully process.

:::
