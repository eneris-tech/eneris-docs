# Settings

The **Settings** page allows you to update the name and email associated with your Eneris account. It also provides information on your subscription details and a few other details.

The **Settings** page can be found on the home screen.

<img src={ImageSettings1} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} width={1280} height={1140} alt="Settings page." />

## Name

Select the edit icon to update your name.

## Email

Select the edit icon to update your email.

- A verification email will be sent to the email address you enter.

## Subscription details

Details on your current subscription plan.

## Team & access

Manage your team members and their roles within your Eneris organization. The **Team & access** section allows administrators to add inspectors, assign roles, and control access permissions.

For detailed information on managing multiple inspectors and team access, see [Multi-inspector](/docs/concepts/multi-inspector).

## System preferences

### Theme

Allows you to set the display theme of the Eneris application.

- For the best experience, we recommend leaving the setting on `System`. This automatically matches the display settings of your device.

### App details

Displays the Eneris application version, build number, and patch ID.

### Import account

Allows you to import your Eneris Legacy account. For more information, see [Migrating your account from Eneris Legacy](/docs/getting-started/migrating-your-account-from-Eneris-Legacy).

## Sign out

Signs you out of your current Eneris account.

## Delete my account

Allows you to permanently delete your Eneris account. This action will delete all data associated with your account, including inspections, reports, templates, team data, uploaded media, and billing information.

This action cannot be undone.

To delete your account:

1. From the Settings page, under **Team & access**, select **Manage accounts and roles**.
2. On the **My team** page, select **Edit team settings** in the top-right.

<img src={ImageEditTeamLink} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} alt="My team page with the Edit team settings link highlighted in the top-right." />

3. Scroll to the **Danger zone** section at the bottom of the page and select **Delete team**.

<img src={ImageDangerZone} style={{ maxWidth: '800px', width: '100%' }} alt="Edit team page with the Danger zone section and Delete team button highlighted." />

4. Review the warning in the confirmation dialog, then select **Delete** to confirm.

After confirming, you will be signed out automatically and a notification will let you know that the deletion request was submitted.

:::warning

Deleting the team also deletes the accounts of all other team members. Only users with the **Admin** role can delete the team. For more on team roles, see [Multi-inspector](/docs/concepts/multi-inspector).

:::

:::info

Account deletion requests may take up to 14 days to fully process.

:::
