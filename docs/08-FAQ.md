<details>

<summary>What's the difference between Web reports and PDF reports?</summary>
<p>
A **Web report** is an interactive online version of your report. Recipients open a link in their browser and can search, jump between sections, browse summary tables, and view enlarged images.

A **PDF report** is a static document, ideal for offline reading, printing, and archival. You can publish either or both at the same time. See [Report formats](/docs/concepts/reports#report-formats) for the full comparison.
</p>
</details>

<details>

<summary>How long will inspection reports be available in Eneris?</summary>
<p>
Inspection data is kept indefinitely, unless specifically stated otherwise ([such as account deletion requests](/docs/account/settings#delete-my-account)). We do not delete inspection reports to save storage space.
</p>
</details>

<details>

<summary>Does Eneris share my inspection data and client information with third-parties such as insurance firms or data brokers?</summary>
<p>
No. Eneris is solely dedicated to providing inspectors with quality inspection software and does not have any incentive to share/sell your information.
</p>
</details>
